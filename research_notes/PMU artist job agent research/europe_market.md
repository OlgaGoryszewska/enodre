# PMU / Micropigmentation Artist Job Market: Norway, Spain, Poland (+ academy trainer routes)

Research date: 5 November 2026. Job-board counts are snapshots from that day. Several job boards (InfoJobs, Indeed ES, Pracuj.pl) blocked automated fetching (HTTP 403/456), so counts for those come only from search-result titles. Treat them as indicative.

## Norway (Oslo, Bergen): job volume, employment model, regulation, employers, prices

### Takeaway
Advertised PMU jobs in Norway are very rare: about 4 ads nationwide on NAV and 1 on FINN, none of them in Oslo or Bergen. Each one bundles PMU with other beauty work, and most are part-time. Regulation sits with the municipality, not Mattilsynet: the premises must be approved by the kommune before PMU work starts, while Mattilsynet regulates the inks. The market looks mostly self-employed or owner-run. A senior artist would most likely need her own approved premises, or a room inside an already approved clinic.

  - "Hudterapeut 40 % vikariat", Relax AS, Trondheim, dated 21 Oct 2026
  - Filters: 4 Deltid (part-time), 3 Fast (permanent), 1 Vikariat. No ad states a salary.
- **FINN.no, search "permanent makeup" (5 Nov 2026):** 1 job ad (MK Hudklinikk, Sandnes, posted 27 days earlier). No salary shown. — [finn.no](https://www.finn.no/job/search?q=permanent+makeup)
- **Premises approval:** In Bergen, any cosmetic treatment that penetrates the skin, including permanent makeup and microneedling, needs municipally approved premises ("godkjenningsplikt"). Applications use the form "Søknad om godkjenning av tatoverings- og piercingsstudio". Miljørettet helsevern publishes a list of approved PMU premises for Bergen, Austevoll, Øygarden, Bjørnafjord, Osterøy and Vaksdal. The page was last updated 10 Sep 2026. — [Bergen kommune: permanent makeup, hudpleie og frisør](https://www.bergen.kommune.no/hvaskjer/tema/miljorettet-helseverns-temaside/helsevern-i-virksomheter/permanent-makeup-hudpleie-og-frisor); [Bergen application form](https://www.bergen.kommune.no/innbyggerhjelpen/naring-og-skatt/naring/tilsyn-og-kontroll-med-naringsvirksomhet/soknad-om-godkjenning-av-tatoveringsog-piercingsstudio)
- **Bergen comparison:** Ordinary hairdressing and skin care need only a notification, but PMU needs approval. — [Bergen kommune tatovering og piercing](https://www.bergen.kommune.no/hvaskjer/tema/miljorettet-helseverns-temaside/helsevern-i-virksomheter/tatovering-og-piercing)
- **Bærum:** Premises must be approved before operations start. — [Bærum kommune](https://www.baerum.kommune.no/politikk-og-samfunn/samfunnsutvikling/folkehelse/miljorettet-helsevern/frisor-hudpleie--tatovering--piercing--og-hulltakingsvirksomheter/)
- **Asker:** Approval authority is delegated to the kommuneoverlege (municipal chief physician) under Miljørettet helsevern. — [Asker kommune](https://www.asker.kommune.no/helse-og-omsorg/miljorettet-helsevern/hygienekrav-for-frisor-hudpleie-eller-tatoveringsvirksomhet/)
- **Mattilsynet's role:** Mattilsynet supervises tattoo and PMU inks. Importers and producers of tattoo products must register with it. The hygiene regulation for hairdressing, skin care and tattooing (the "hygieneforskrift") is enforced by the municipalities. — [Mattilsynet: tatoveringsstoff](https://mattilsynet.no/kosmetikk-og-kroppspleieprodukter/tatoveringsstoff-skal-vere-trygge-i-bruk); [Mattilsynet: varsle om tatoveringsprodukter](https://mattilsynet.no/skjemaer/varsle-om-tatoveringsprodukter)
- **Prices:** Klikk.no gives NOK 4,000–6,000 per PMU or microblading treatment and NOK 1,000–2,000 per touch-up. The article is undated and may be old. — [Klikk.no](https://www.klikk.no/mote/skjonnhet/microblading-2734035)
- **Oslo clinic example:** Oslo Cosmetic Laser Center lists permanent makeup from NOK 6,000 (directory cache from June 2025). — [WhatClinic](https://www.whatclinic.com/beauty-clinics/norway/oslo/oslo-cosmetic-laser-center)
- **Oslo supply example:** Home of Beauty AS (Majorstuen) is a small salon specialising in PMU, microblading and non-laser PMU removal. — [Gulesider](https://www.gulesider.no/home+of+beauty+as/125121470/bedrift)
- **Quality warning:** Klikk notes that some Norwegian providers use photos of foreign artists' work, so a verifiable portfolio matters. — [Klikk.no](https://www.klikk.no/mote/skjonnhet/microblading-2734035)

### Inferences
- With roughly 4 to 5 ads a year at any one time, and none in Oslo or Bergen, a job agent that relies on NAV or FINN alone will find almost nothing. Better channels would be direct outreach to clinics on the municipal approved-premises lists (Bergen publishes one) and to medical-aesthetics clinics, plus Instagram.
- Employers that do advertise expect a combined role (PMU plus brows, nails, waxing or reception), usually part-time. That points to a mostly self-employed (ENK) market or room rental inside approved premises. No source confirms rental or commission norms.
- Because approval is tied to premises rather than to the practitioner, an incoming artist can work legally inside an already approved clinic without obtaining her own approval.
- At NOK 4,000–6,000 per procedure, 10 to 15 new clients a month would gross roughly NOK 40,000–90,000 before costs and rent. This is arithmetic only, not a sourced earnings figure.

### Gaps
- Could not load the hygieneforskrift text on Lovdata (the URL tried returned 404). Exact rules on staff competence and approval criteria were not verified.
- No public vacancies or careers pages were verified for Akademiklinikken, Klinikk Bryn, Dr. Arvind or similar clinics. Do not assume these clinics hire PMU artists or publish careers emails.
- No source found for Norwegian employed salaries, commission percentages or chair/room rental rates for PMU.
- Instagram hiring could not be measured.

## Spain (Madrid, Barcelona, Marbella/Costa del Sol, Mallorca): regulation, job boards, employers, pay, English-speaking clinics

### Takeaway
Micropigmentation is regulated region by region. Each autonomous community has its own decree requiring hygienic-sanitary training and authorisation or registration of the establishment. On top of that, a new state-level Real Decreto 488/2026 (BOE, 19 Jun 2026) creates a 510-hour level-3 professional certificate in micropigmentation techniques. Advertised jobs are few: InfoJobs search titles show only 2 to 3 offers nationally. Published pay is low, around €1,500–2,500 gross a month on salary. No English-speaking Costa del Sol vacancy was found.

### Cited Findings
- **State list of regional decrees:** The Ministerio de Sanidad publishes a list of each region's decrees on tattooing, micropigmentation and piercing. — [Sanidad: Listado Decretos Autonómicos](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/ciudadanos/enfLesiones/enfTransmisibles/hepatitisC/estadisticas/docs/Listado_DecretosAutonomicos_tatuajes_piercing_yotrastecnicas.pdf)
- **Madrid:** Decreto 35/2005, de 10 de marzo, regulates tattooing, micropigmentation, piercing and similar practices. — [Comunidad de Madrid BVCM](https://gestiona3.madrid.org/bvirtual/BVCM009419.pdf); [Madrid Salud report](https://www.madrid.es/UnidadesDescentralizadas/Salud/Publicaciones%20Propias%20Madrid%20salud/InformesSaludP%C3%BAblica/ficheros/Cons09010%20-%20CT-09-00190.pdf)
- **Cataluña:** Decreto 90/2008, de 22 de abril, covers tattoo, micropigmentation and piercing practices and their hygienic-sanitary requirements. Whether it has since been amended was not verified. — [Sanidad list](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/ciudadanos/enfLesiones/enfTransmisibles/hepatitisC/estadisticas/docs/Listado_DecretosAutonomicos_tatuajes_piercing_yotrastecnicas.pdf)
- **Andalucía (Marbella / Costa del Sol):** Decreto 71/2017, de 13 de junio, later amended. Practitioners had to meet its training requirements by 1 March 2022. — [CES Andalucía Dictamen 6/2020](https://cdn.juntadeandalucia.es/sites/default/files/inline-files/2023/03/1_2360_Dictamen_6-2020.pdf)
- **Mallorca (Illes Balears):** The decree was not individually verified. Check the Sanidad list.
- **Typical training hours:** The hygienic-sanitary course is commonly 35 hours.
  - Basque Country (Lanbide) module: 26 h on health and hygiene plus 9 h practical. — [Lanbide](https://web.lanbide.eus/apps/FR_Descarga_Archivo?IDRG=34973)
  - A commercial course "homologated" for Madrid and Barcelona: 35 h (10 h theory in person, 9 h practical, 16 h distance). This is an academy's claim, not decree text. — [Educaweb](https://www.educaweb.com/curso/higienico-sanitario-196163/)
  - Aragón publishes rules on cross-recognition of these courses between regions. — [Aragón PDF](https://www.aragon.es/documents/20127/674325/CONVALIDACION_Y_RECONOCIMIENTO_MUTUO_CURSOS.pdf/8a260298-87b2-e6f8-49f0-6cb5133bf836)
- **New state certificate (2026):** Real Decreto 488/2026, de 17 de junio, establishes the "Certificado profesional en Procedimientos y técnicas de micropigmentación". — [BOE-A-2026-13294](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/06/19/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-13294.pdf)
  - Familia profesional: Imagen Personal. Level 3. Duration: 510 hours.
  - Modules: 1422 Seguridad y salud en procesos de micropigmentación, tatuaje y piercing; 1423 Asesoría; 1424 Procesos de micropigmentación; 1761 Renovación, modificación y eliminación de micropigmentación.
  - Listed occupations: micropigmentation specialists; advisers to micropigmentation schools and academies; directors of micropigmentation centres.
  - Trainers must hold the "certificado profesional de Habilitación para la docencia".
  - Entry into force: 20 days after publication, so about 9 July 2026.
  - Whether the certificate is mandatory for practice, or only an optional accreditation, was not confirmed from the extracted text.
- **InfoJobs volume:** Search-result titles showed "2 ofertas de micropigmentadora en todas las ubicaciones" and "3 ofertas de micropigmentacion cejas en todas las ubicaciones". The pages could not be fetched (HTTP 456), so the date of these counts is unknown. — [InfoJobs micropigmentadora](https://www.infojobs.net/ofertas-trabajo/micropigmentadora); [InfoJobs micropigmentacion cejas](https://www.infojobs.net/ofertas-trabajo/micropigmentacion-cejas)
- **Individual InfoJobs ads in search results:** "Micropigmentadora" in Barcelona and "Técnico de Micropigmentación" in Valencia. Details could not be fetched. — [Barcelona ad](https://www.infojobs.net/barcelona/micropigmentadora/of-i898d81ce754e2bacd9c4fa33cc7588); [Valencia ad](https://www.infojobs.net/valencia/tecnico-micropigmentacion/of-i34d041a7994ecead43c331b77c8420)
- **Pay:**
  - Average of about €1,930/month for a micropigmentation specialist, attributed to Indeed by a training-sales site. Low reliability. — [Campus Training](https://www.campustraining.es/cursos/estetica/tratamientos-esteticos-micropigmentacion-tatuajes/sueldo/)
  - An InfoJobs aesthetics ad in Oviedo offered €1,500–2,500 gross/month, indefinido, full-time. — [InfoJobs Oviedo](https://www.infojobs.net/oviedo/esteticista/of-i841b53832149e299bafddf1a58da44)
- **Collective agreement:** The state convenio for hairdressing, aesthetics and beauty centres applies nationwide to salaried staff. — [BOE-A-2025-1624](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2025/01/29/pdfs/BOE-A-2025-1624.pdf); [BOE-A-2021-19455](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2021/11/25/pdfs/BOE-A-2021-19455.pdf)
- **Marbella employers (directory listings, no vacancies found):**
  - 4D Pigmentación Studio (hair, brow and reconstructive micropigmentation)
  - Tamara López Micropigmentación (treatments plus training courses)
  - Clínica Dr. Campos (plastic surgery; staff speak Spanish, English, French and Russian)
  - WhatClinic lists only 3 PMU clinics in Marbella.
  - Sources: [WhatClinic Marbella PMU](https://www.whatclinic.com/beauty-clinics/spain/marbella/permanent-makeup); [Tressless: Tamara López](https://tressless.com/smp/tamara-lopez); [Fresha Marbella powder brows](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/tt/powder-brows/in/es-marbella)
- **Recruitment agency lead:** birecruit.com has job tags for micropigmentation and microblading. Its geography and current listings were not verified. — [birecruit micropigmentation](https://birecruit.com/job-tag/micropigmentation/)

### Inferences
- A non-Spanish senior artist will probably need two things: the regional hygienic-sanitary course of about 35 hours (each region can recognise other regions' courses), and premises authorised by the regional health department. The new 510-hour certificate may become the benchmark qualification for employers and for academy trainer roles. Its "advisers to academies" occupation is relevant to a trainer path.
- Salaried pay under the beauty convenio is low, so self-employment (autónoma) or commission inside clinics is likely the better earning route for a senior artist. No source confirms commission rates.
- On the Costa del Sol, clinics with multilingual staff, such as surgery clinics serving international clients, are the most plausible employers for an English-speaking artist. This rests on a single data point and no vacancy was found.

### Gaps
- InfoJobs and Indeed ES counts and ad details could not be fetched (blocked). City-level counts for Madrid, Barcelona, Marbella and Mallorca are unknown.
- Mallorca (Balearic) decree specifics and training hours were not verified.
- No verified hiring evidence for English-speaking international clinics. No careers emails were found.
- No sourced commission or room-rental rates for Spain.

## Poland (Warsaw, Kraków): market, listings, pay, rules

### Takeaway
Dedicated linergistka jobs are scarce on job boards. OLX returns 71 results for "linergistka", but almost all are unrelated; only about 2 are genuine PMU roles, and none are in Kraków. Contracts mix umowa o pracę (employment), zlecenie (contract of mandate) and B2B or self-employment. There is no PMU-specific licence. The rules are GIS/Sanepid hygiene requirements for the premises plus EU REACH rules on inks.

### Cited Findings
- **OLX "linergistka", Praca category (5 Nov 2026):** 71 results (40 shown on page 1). — [OLX](https://www.olx.pl/praca/q-linergistka/)
  - Genuine PMU-related ads: "Kosmetyczka/linergistka", Warszawa-Wola, umowa o pracę, no pay listed; and "Ligernistka, stylistka rzęs", Chełmno, umowa o pracę, listed at 4,950–70,000 zł/month (an implausible range, likely a data-entry error).
  - The rest are mostly unrelated ads: receptionists, couriers, Hebe drugstore staff.
  - Aesthetic-clinic receptionist ads in Warsaw pay 4,000–5,000 zł/month, which helps calibrate local beauty-sector pay.
- **Pracuj.pl:** Direct search was blocked (403). One PMU studio employer profile was found: "Makijaż Permanentny & Kosmetologia Estetyczna Monika Dąbrowska" (Elbląg). Its listed role was a nail stylist on zlecenie or B2B. — [Pracuj.pl employer profile](https://pracodawcy.pracuj.pl/company/1074243744)
- **Training:** The PARP state training registry lists many subsidised linergistka courses. One example is a 38-hour basic course with exam, described as preparing participants to work independently. This suggests a large supply of newly trained artists. — [PARP usługi rozwojowe example](https://uslugirozwojowe.parp.gov.pl/wyszukiwarka/uslugi/drukuj-pdf?id=2711674)
- **Rules:** The GIS (Sanepid) published "Zalecenia i wymagania higieniczno-sanitarne Głównego Inspektora Sanitarnego w zakresie świadczenia usług w branży beauty", citing Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1675. Invasive aesthetic-medicine procedures reserved for medical staff fall under a separate Health Ministry regulation of 26 March 2019. — [gov.pl GIS document](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/eacabf2f-a954-4f38-999a-d74b7f10d96f)
- **Premises:** Beauty and tattoo premises must meet the Infrastructure Ministry regulation of 12 April 2002 on technical conditions. — [gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/21fed645-d0e2-4882-9bbc-bc9cd7fa3f61)
- **Inks:** GIS points to EU Commission rules (REACH restriction) on tattoo and PMU inks. — [Infor.pl](https://www.infor.pl/prawo/prawa-konsumenta/nowosci/5466410,Makijaz-permanentny-lub-tatuaz-z-odpowiednim-tuszem-radzi-GIS.html)

### Inferences
- The Polish market looks saturated with self-employed linergistki, fed by cheap subsidised courses. Salaried PMU vacancies are rare, and Kraków showed none.
- For a senior artist, Poland's realistic value lies in trainer or academy work, or in own-studio work under Sanepid-compliant premises, rather than in employment.

### Gaps
- No reliable source on linergistka earnings or commission percentages. The search returned irrelevant results.
- Pracuj.pl counts and PhiAcademy-linked Polish studio hiring were not verified.
- The exact Sanepid opinion or approval procedure for opening a PMU studio was not confirmed from primary text.

## PMU academies and brands that take experienced artists as trainers or masters (Europe/GCC)

### Takeaway
PhiAcademy, the parent of PhiBrows and Nanobrows-style techniques, does not advertise trainer jobs. Its path to "Master" status is an internal rank ladder that requires being inside the Phi system first. Experienced outside artists cannot be hired straight in as trainers.

### Cited Findings
- **PhiAcademy ladder:** Student → Artist (pass the Craftmaster app lessons) → at least 6 months as Artist → Royal (via Perfection training) → Master Associate (a set number of supervised monitoring sessions) → Grand Master (new techniques plus recognition in the industry). Royals, Masters and Grand Masters can assist students. The page gives no application process or earnings details and dates from 4 Nov 2021. — [Phi Academy blog](https://phi-academy.com/en-us/blog/how-to-become-master-at-phi-academy)
- **Older PhiAcademy pages:**
  - 10 monitoring sessions to become Master Assistant; Master Assistants are paid to help Craft Masters train students.
  - Master Technician can become Grand Master after more than 100 certified students in a year and more than 1 year in the role.
  - These pages are older and may be outdated, and they conflict with the 2021 blog's naming.
  - Sources: [old.phi-academy.com](https://old.phi-academy.com/?p=25479); [old.phi-academy.com/?p=48](https://old.phi-academy.com/?p=48)
- **PhiAcademy offers** PhiBrows, Nanobrows, PhiContour and powder brows courses globally. — [Phi Academy Nanobrows course](https://phi-academy.com/en-us/courses/nanobrows)

### Inferences
- For a senior artist trained outside Phi, the Phi trainer route means retraining in Phi techniques and climbing the ranks over at least 1 to 2 years. Independent academies, such as Spain's new certificate-based training centres or local PMU schools, may be a faster route to trainer work.

### Gaps
- No vacancy or careers email was found for PhiAcademy or other brands (Nanobrows academies, Li Pigments and others) recruiting trainers in Europe or the GCC. The GCC was not researched due to the tool budget.
- No beauty or aesthetics recruitment agencies were verified for Norway or Poland. For Spain, only birecruit.com turned up, unverified.
- No careers emails were found anywhere in this research, and none are listed.
