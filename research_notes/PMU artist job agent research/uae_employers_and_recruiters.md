# UAE employers and recruiters for semi-permanent makeup (PMU) / micropigmentation artists

Research date: 5 November 2026. Scope: Dubai first, then Abu Dhabi. I made about 18 search and fetch calls, so coverage is partial. Many brands named in the brief (Sisters Beauty Lounge, Tips & Toes, NStyle, Bedashing, Eclat, The Lounge, Shiseido, Kaya, Euromed, CosmeSurge, Dr. Kayle, Biolite, Aesthetics International, Mediclinic, Aster) were **not** confirmed as offering PMU, because I did not reach their own service menus. The notes say which entries are verified and which are not.

## What is "Her Place" in Dubai? Website, branches, PMU menu, how it recruits

### Takeaway
Her Place ("HER PLACE – Exclusive Beauty with German Quality") is a small, new ladies' beauty salon in Jumeirah Village Circle. It is run by a team with German beauty-industry experience and joined Fresha in March 2026. Its visible menu is lash extensions. It lists no website, and no PMU service or recruitment channel could be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Address: Street 5, District 16, The Vybe by Metrical Development, Jumeirah Village Circle, Dubai. Its category is "Ladies Beauty Salon". — [Fresha: Her Place](https://www.fresha.com/es-MX/p/her-place-6245359)
- The Fresha profile says the salon is built on "years of beauty industry experience in Germany" and lists English and German as its languages. It joined Fresha in March 2026, has 4 completed appointments and has no reviews. — [Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/es-MX/p/her-place-6245359)
- The profile shows 30 services. Only 6 are visible, and all are lash services: classic 1D lashes (AED 179–350), soft volume (AED 199), Russian volume (AED 219), refills, and removal (AED 120). No PMU, brow or lip tattooing is visible. One portfolio caption mentions "plumped lips", which may mean filler or lip styling rather than PMU. — [Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/es-MX/p/her-place-6245359)
- Contact channels: Instagram @herplace.dxb (instagram.com/herplace.dxb) and facebook.com/herplace. No website and no phone number are listed. — [Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/es-MX/p/her-place-6245359)

### Inferences
- The salon has one branch and appears to have one staff member ("1 professional" on the booking page). It is a very early-stage business. It could be a good fit for a German-speaking, European-trained PMU artist who wants to add PMU to its menu, but it is not an established employer. The only realistic contact route is an Instagram DM.
- A different Dubai business could use a similar name. The user should confirm that this is the "Her Place" they meant.

### Gaps
- The full 30-service menu was not visible, so PMU may be among the 24 hidden services.
- Its recruitment method and any careers email are unknown. No job posts were found.

The verified PMU employers fall into three groups:
- Specialist brow and PMU studios: Brau., Browz, Browns & Co, Brows & Lips, PhiStudio and Permanent Makeup Center.
- Medical or surgical clinics offering PMU and medical tattooing: Novomed, Al Shunnar Plastic Surgery, American Hospital Dubai, Lespirit Medical Clinic and The Private Clinic. These are the best fit for a senior artist with medical-clinic experience.

## Do medical clinics hire PMU artists as "beauty therapist" or "aesthetic technician", and does that change the licence?

### Takeaway
Yes, in practice. Clinics advertise "beauty therapist", "medical aesthetician", "beauty/laser therapist" or "skincare specialist" roles that require a **DHA licence**, and PMU job ads at salons also demand a DHA licence. I found no primary DHA or Dubai Municipality regulation that states which authority licenses PMU in which setting.

### Cited Findings
- PMU job ads require a DHA licence: Royal Retreat (DHA licence a "MUST", plus a PMU certificate and 5 years of experience) and Hamzafied ("DHA licensed semi-permanent and microblading artist"). — [Qureos](https://app.qureos.com/jobs/permanant-make-up-artist-microblading-lip-blush-eyeliner-1758139923996); [Eurostaffs](https://eurostaffs.org/job/dha-licensed-semi-permanent-and-microblading-artist-hamzafied-beauty-salon)
- Clinics hire under titles such as "Medical Aesthetician / Beauty Therapist", "Beauty/Laser Therapist" and "Nurses – Beauty/Laser Therapist". — [MNC Jobs Gulf](https://www.mncjobsgulf.com/jobs/medical-aesthetician-beauty-therapist-dubai-1804106); [Medbiomate (Kaya)](https://www.medbiomate.com/job/nurses-beauty-laser-therapist)
- Novomed titles its PMU providers "skincare specialist". — [Novomed](https://novomed.com/?p=19193)
- Brows & Lips Academy says graduates apply for a PMU licence through "DHA or Dubai Municipality depending on the setting", and that KHDA certifies the training but does not license practitioners. This is marketing copy. — [browsandlips.ae](https://browsandlips.ae/blog/pmu-training-courses-in-dubai-microblading-lip-blush-certification-khda-approved/)
- A Dubai Municipality document refers to a "Semi-Permanent Makeup Technician / tattoo engraving (microblading) technician" activity and cites an Abu Dhabi Quality and Conformity Council classification. Only a snippet was seen. — [DM/DMT PDF](https://pages.dmt.gov.ae/-/media/142F6293A49D46AF93FAD6A21EC5122A.ashx)
- A business advisory firm notes that a standard DET salon licence covers core personal care, and that some services need a separate DHA permit. — [DIAC](https://diac.ae/blog/beauty-salon-business-dubai-uae/)

### Inferences
- Working inside a DHA-licensed medical facility, especially on areola, scar or SMP work, very likely requires a DHA professional licence under a beauty-therapist-type category. Salon work may fall under Dubai Municipality technician registration. Abu Dhabi would fall under DoH Abu Dhabi, which was not researched.

### Gaps
- No primary DHA Professional Qualification Requirements (PQR) text was found for PMU or beauty therapists. The exact licence category, exam and evidence needed are unverified.
- DoH Abu Dhabi rules for PMU and beauty therapists were not researched.
