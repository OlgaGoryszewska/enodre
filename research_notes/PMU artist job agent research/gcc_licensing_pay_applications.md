# GCC Licensing, Visas, Pay and Application Norms for a Senior PMU / Micropigmentation Artist (UAE first, then KSA and Qatar)

Research date: 5 November 2026. Each item is tagged **[OFFICIAL]** (a government or regulator document), **[COMMERCIAL]** (a consultancy, training school or job board) or **[ANECDOTAL]**. Two official PDFs were downloaded and read in full text: the DHA Non-Surgical Cosmetic Standard v2.1 and the DHA Licensing Manual v1.1. The Abu Dhabi DMT Circular 6/2024 was also downloaded and read in full text.

## 1. Licensing in Dubai (DHA vs Dubai Municipality / DED-DET salon permits)

### Takeaway
In Dubai, semi-permanent makeup, microblading and PMU removal (including eyebrow and lip tattoo) are listed DHA-regulated non-surgical cosmetic procedures. The PMU artist needs a personal DHA professional licence under the **Beauty Therapist** title (DHA does not use "PMU artist" as a title). A Beauty Therapist may perform PMU in a salon or other non-clinical setting without medical supervision. A trade or DET salon licence alone, or a training certificate alone, is not enough. The process is Sheryan self-assessment, DataFlow PSV, a Prometric CBT if required, then DHA registration, which is valid for 1 year. You can complete all of that without a job offer. Only the final licence **activation** needs an employer.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** The DHA "Standards for Non-Surgical Cosmetic Procedures" (code DHA/HRS/HPSD/ST-05, v2.1, issued 18/12/2024, effective 18/02/2025, revision due 2029) has an Appendix 1 table. Row 28 covers "Semi-permanent Makeup, Micro-blading and removal of semi-permanent makeup including eyebrow and lip tattoo". It is permitted for Dermatologist/Plastic Surgeon ✓, Other physicians/GP ✓ and **Beauty Therapist ✓**. It is **not** permitted for Nurse ✗, Physiotherapist ✗ or Massage Therapist ✗. — [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Clause 7.11.3 of the same standard: "Beauty therapist and massage therapist may perform limited number of non-surgical procedures listed in Appendix 1, in a salon or a non-clinical setting without the supervision of a healthcare professional". The listed exceptions are laser hair removal/IPL and microneedling, which still need a clinical setting. PMU is **not** an exception. — [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Clause 7.11 sets three extra conditions:
  - The therapist works where "prescribed by a DHA licensed Consultant/Specialist Dermatologist or Consultant/Specialist Plastic Surgeon and in accordance to their written instructions and treatment plan".
  - The therapist holds "a recognised specific in-person training certificate from an established institution recognized by an authorized accrediting body in the same country" (7.11.1). Standard 5 adds that the certificate should state the number of CPD hours (9.3).
  - The therapist obtains a privilege from the CPC (7.11.2).
  - Note: 7.11 (prescription by a derm/plastic surgeon) and 7.11.3 (no supervision needed in a salon) read as being in tension. How salons apply this in practice is unverified. — [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Experience: "All healthcare professionals coming from abroad should have a minimum of two (2) years' experience performing non-surgical cosmetic procedures… 7.3.1 Beauty Therapist require a minimum of one (1) year experience." — [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL]** In a non-surgical cosmetic health facility, professionals must hold an active DHA licence, a valid BLS certificate and "up to date medical malpractice insurance" (7.1.1–7.1.3). — [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL] (important for CV and cover-letter wording)** Clause 7.2: licensed professionals "shall refrain from using any other names or speciality titles that is different from their DHA license such as … Aesthetician, Cosmetologist, Beauty Specialist or Consultant, … Cosmetic or Beauty Expert etc." — [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL]** The DHA "Manual for Licensing Healthcare Professionals" (DHA/HRS/HLD/MA-2 v1.1, issued 20/11/2024) gives these steps (5.6):
  1. Create a Sheryan account and do the self-assessment.
  2. Fulfil the PQR requirements (qualification, experience, home-country licence, good standing).
  3. Get a positive Primary Source Verification (PSV).
  4. Pass the assessment (CBT or oral).
  5. Professional Registration Certificate is issued.
  6. "Activate HP license by an employer who should hold a valid DHA license as a health facility". — [DHA Licensing Manual PDF](https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Manual%20for%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20professionals-202420241212775.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL]** In the same manual, registration "confirms the HP eligibility… HP registration must be activated into a license by a hiring facility in order to start" practice. "The validity of the registration is one year from the date of issuance" (10.12). "Both PSV and CBT steps can be done simultaneously" (10.5). PSV documents are qualifications, experience certificates, licences/registrations, and a "Good standing certificate not older than 6 months" (10.3). Non-English/Arabic originals need certified legal translation (5.7). Applicants who have failed three times across the UAE authorities are barred (10.13). — [DHA Licensing Manual PDF](https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Manual%20for%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20professionals-202420241212775.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL]** The Sheryan Self-Assessment Tool (service code MPQR) checks eligibility against the Unified PQR. — [DHA Sheryan](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/portal/home/services-professional/service-description?scode=MPQR&CATALOGUE_TYPE=PROFESSIONAL)
- **[COMMERCIAL, "Updated January 2026"]** The DHA title is "Aesthetician / Beauty Therapist" (allied health).
  - Qualification: a diploma in beauty therapy/cosmetics from a nationally accredited institute, "minimum one (1) year course duration".
  - Experience: 1 year post-qualification. Also needed: home-country licence where applicable, and a Good Standing Certificate dated within 6 months.
  - Prometric CBT: code BYT5862, 2 hours, 100 questions, 65% pass mark, booked via prometric.com/dha, retake after 14 days. The syllabus is anatomy/physiology, health & safety, electrotherapy, microneedling, skin care, peels/Hydrafacial, RF and ethics. **It has no PMU-specific domain.**
  - Fees: DataFlow AED 935 for 4 documents, plus AED 300 per extra document, plus 5% VAT. Eligibility letter AED 200. Exam USD 180 (the page also says USD 240; not reconciled).
  - Timeline: DataFlow 3–6 weeks.
  - English requirement if not educated in English: IELTS 5.0 / OET C.
  - Exam exemptions: possible for licences from the UK (HCPC), Germany, Australia etc. Norway is not listed. — [mydoctorlicense.com](https://mydoctorlicense.com/dha-license-for-aesthetician-beauty-therapist/)
- **[COMMERCIAL]** Conflict: a Dubai beauty institute says Beauty Therapist applicants need a minimum of **2 years** of experience, which may be waived for graduates of KHDA/DHA-approved Dubai schools. The DHA standard says 1 year for beauty therapists. — [Furless Beauty Institute](https://furlessbeautyinstitute.com/blogs/articles/how-to-obtain-a-beauty-therapy-dha-license-in-dubai); [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)
- **[COMMERCIAL]** A new DataFlow report takes 15 working days from payment. A report transferred from another UAE authority takes 5 working days. — [DHA License consultancy summary via search](https://www.overseashealthcareconsultant.com/dha-license/)
- **[COMMERCIAL / UNVERIFIED]** A Dubai PMU studio site says a **March 2026 Dubai Municipality notice**:
  - requires salons and beauty centres "to obtain the necessary DHA licences" for non-surgical cosmetic services;
  - says such services are "prohibited unless performed by professionals with an approved DHA professional licence";
  - states that "Training alone is not a substitute for an active licence".
  - I could not find the notice itself on dm.gov.ae. — [microblading.ae](https://www.microblading.ae/)
- **[COMMERCIAL]** Salon-setup consultancies say a standard DET (ex-DED) beauty salon licence covers core personal-care services. They say tattooing/permanent makeup needs separate DHA approval, with approval taking about 2–6 weeks. — [DIAC blog](https://diac.ae/blog/beauty-salon-business-dubai-uae/); [Sarmat](https://sarmat.ae/resources/freelance-makeup-artist-license-dubai)
- **[COMMERCIAL job ad]** A Jumeirah ladies' salon (Royal Retreat Beauty Center) PMU ad says "DHA license is a MUST". It also asks for an accredited PMU certificate, 5 years' experience, a portfolio, colour theory and English. — [Qureos listing (expired)](https://app.qureos.com/jobs/permanant-make-up-artist-microblading-lip-blush-eyeliner-1758139923996)
- **[OFFICIAL]** The DHA Medical Registry (public lookup of licensed professionals) is where employers verify a candidate. — [DHA professional registry](https://www.dha.gov.ae/en/medical-listing/professionals)

### Inferences
- The right Dubai target is the **DHA Beauty Therapist** licence. A Norwegian PMU artist with no recognised 1-year beauty-therapy diploma may fail the PQR qualification test. That is the main eligibility risk, and running the Sheryan self-assessment is the first concrete step.
- The CBT syllabus is general aesthetics, not PMU, so a PMU-only artist needs exam preparation.
- The candidate can reach "DHA registered (eligibility)" status **before** any job offer, with 12 months of validity. The employer is only needed for activation. Being registered makes the candidate much more hireable, because ads say "DHA license is a MUST" or "DHA eligibility".
- Who pays for DataFlow and the exam is not set by DHA. In practice the candidate usually pays pre-offer costs, and some employers pay or reimburse activation costs. This is unverified; see Gaps.
- SMP and medical tattooing (areola, scar camouflage) are not listed separately in Appendix 1. They likely fall under clinic and derm/plastic-surgeon scope. Unverified.

### Gaps
- I did not find an official DHA PQR page listing the exact Beauty Therapist qualification and experience text. The Unified PQR PDF (2022) is linked from the manual but was not read.
- I could not find a primary source for the March 2026 Dubai Municipality notice.
- I found no source on whether a PMU-only certificate (e.g. PhiBrows Academy) satisfies the "recognised in-person training… accredited body in the same country" rule.
- I found no source on who typically pays licence costs (candidate vs employer).
- Total realistic timeline is an inference, not sourced: DataFlow 3–6 weeks + exam + registration + activation ≈ 2–4 months.

## 2. Abu Dhabi (DOH) and Northern Emirates (MOHAP)

### Takeaway
In Abu Dhabi, salon microblading/PMU is handled outside DOH. The salon needs a DED "tattoo engraving" activity licence, and **each technician needs a certificate of conformity from the Abu Dhabi Quality and Conformity Council (QCC)** under the occupation "Semi-Permanent Makeup Technician". This comes from DMT Circular No. 6 of 2024. The DOH Beauty Therapist licence is commonly requested for clinic roles. I found nothing on MOHAP.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** Abu Dhabi DMT Circular No. (6) of 2024, "Concerning the Activity of Tattoo Engraving (Microblading) in Beauty Centres and Hairdressing Salons" (PDF metadata dated 7 Feb 2025). It says: "All beauty centers and hair salons having a tattoo engraving activity license issued by the Department of Economic Development must obtain a certificate of conformity for technicians working in the activity of Semi-Permanent Makeup Technician/ tattoo engraving (microblading) technician from the Abu Dhabi Quality and Conformity Council." Its legal basis is Law No. 2 of 2012 (health control of non-food establishments). — [DMT Circular PDF](https://pages.dmt.gov.ae/-/media/142F6293A49D46AF93FAD6A21EC5122A.ashx)
- **[OFFICIAL]** The QCC publishes "Abu Dhabi Occupational Terms for Semi-Permanent Makeup" (first edition, 2024). Found via search; the PDF was not read. — [QCC document](https://qcc.uacuae.com/admin/document/doc_file/doc_file_202407160729.pdf)
- **[COMMERCIAL job ads]** Abu Dhabi beauty/aesthetic job ads ask for a "DOH beauty therapist licence" or "DOH/DHA eligibility" for facials, derma pen and similar work. None tie DOH to microblading. — [MNC Jobs Gulf](https://www.mncjobsgulf.com/jobs/beauty-therapist-doh-dha-eligibility-abu-dhabi-1795788); [Qureos](https://app.qureos.com/jobs/facial-therapist-doh-licensed-1770473757505)

### Inferences
- For an Abu Dhabi salon role, the cover letter should mention willingness to obtain the **QCC Semi-Permanent Makeup Technician conformity certificate**. A DOH licence matters only for clinic-based roles.
- Holding a DHA licence does not automatically give Abu Dhabi salon eligibility. UAE healthcare licences transfer between DHA/DOH/MOHAP with a fast PSV transfer, but the QCC route is separate.

### Gaps
- QCC certificate requirements, fees and assessment format were not found (QCC PDF not read).
- MOHAP's rules for PMU in the northern emirates (Sharjah, Ajman etc.) are unknown. No source found.

## 3. Saudi Arabia (SCFHS vs municipal) and Qatar (MoPH/DHP vs municipal)

### Takeaway
I found no official source confirming that SCFHS or Qatar's MoPH licenses salon PMU artists. Evidence is thin and partly commercial. Saudi women's salons operate under a municipal licence. Saudi barbershop (men's) rules ban tattoo equipment. A training company claims Qatar has no specific licence for cosmetic tattooing. Treat both countries as "verify with the employer and the authority before claiming anything in a letter".

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL text via legal database, not fully read: 403]** Saudi "Regulations for the Practice of Women's Beauty Activities" require a municipal licence. Changing activity or location requires municipal approval. — [Qanoniah](https://qanoniah.com/en/File/DmWqyAxNgpoP8NKoP26zvBXaE-Regulations-for-the-Practice-of-Women's-Beauty-Activities)
- **[NEWS, 2024]** The Saudi Ministry of Municipalities and Housing banned tanning beds and tattoo paraphernalia in **barbershops** (men's), required sterilisation devices, and allowed only SFDA-approved cosmetics. This applies to barbershops, not women's salons. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-bans-tattoos-tanning-at-barbershops-1.103282231); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-tightens-barbershop-regulations-1.1731145761390)
- **[ACADEMIC/RELIGIOUS]** One Shariah-perspective paper and a fatwa body consider permanent makeup impermissible. This is a cultural sensitivity, not a licensing rule. Note that PMU is widely sold in Riyadh and Jeddah (many Fresha listings). — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/383832541_PERMANENT_MAKEUP_A_TATTOO_IMITATION_PROCEDURE_FROM_THE_SHARIAH_PERSPECTIVE); [Fresha Riyadh](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/tt/permanent-makeup-tattooing/in/sa-al-riyadh/6125)
- **[COMMERCIAL, undated, ©2025]** "There are currently no specific licensing requirements in Qatar to provide cosmetic tattoo services" (permanent makeup and microblading). The page adds that local health and safety rules may apply. It names no authority. — [NIOB Qatar](https://www.nationalinstituteofbeauty.com/regulations/qatar/)
- **[COMMERCIAL]** Doha PMU prices run about QAR 400–2,000 per treatment. — [Fresha Doha](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/tt/permanent-makeup-tattooing/in/qa-doha)

### Inferences
- In Saudi Arabia, SCFHS registration is unlikely to apply to salon PMU unless the role is inside a medical complex or dermatology clinic. In that case, SCFHS classification (via Mumaris+ and DataFlow) would likely be required. Unverified.
- Saudi women's-salon roles are female-only environments. Cultural sensitivity around "tattoo" wording suggests letters should use "semi-permanent makeup / micropigmentation" rather than "tattoo". This is inference.

### Gaps
- No SCFHS, MoMAH or Qatar MoPH/DHP primary source was found. An Arabic-language search is needed (e.g. لائحة اشتراطات مشاغل التجميل النسائية).
- Qatar Ministry of Municipality salon permit rules for PMU were not found.

## 4. Visas (UAE employment visa, Emirates ID, medical, red flags)

### Takeaway
UAE work visas are employer-sponsored. The employer must pay visa costs by law; this is commercial-sourced, and the law is Federal Decree-Law 33/2021. The sequence is entry permit, medical fitness test, Emirates ID, then residence visa. Working on a visit visa, or "own visa / husband's sponsorship only" arrangements, are red flags for a relocating artist.

### Cited Findings
- **[COMMERCIAL]** "UAE law prohibits charging employees for visa costs. The employer bears all expenses." — [Hyring HR glossary](https://hyring.com/free-hr-toolkit/hr-glossary/employment-visa-uae)
- **[COMMERCIAL]** The employer's all-in cost is estimated at AED 3,000–7,000, or AED 6,000–10,000 per skilled employee depending on free zone and guarantee. The medical test costs about AED 300–500 and is mandatory. Emirates ID costs about AED 370 for 2 years and should be applied for within about 15 days of arrival. The entry permit gives about 60 days to complete the medical and Emirates ID steps. — [SPC Free Zone](https://www.spcfz.ae/blogs/uae-employment-visa-requirements/); [FMC Group](https://fmcgroup.com/uae-work-visa-sponsorship-guide/); [Shuraa](https://www.shuraa.com/dubai-work-visa-permit)
- **[COMMERCIAL job ad]** Some Dubai SPMU ads require the candidate to be on her "own visa (husband/father sponsorship)", meaning no employer sponsorship. — [Indeed UAE](https://ae.indeed.com/q-permanent-makeup-l-dubai-free-zone-jobs.html)

### Inferences
- The agent should flag ads saying "own visa", "freelance visa required" or "commission only" as unsuitable for relocation from Norway.
- A DHA licence activation needs a health-facility employer, and the work visa needs a sponsor. Ideally they are the same employer.

### Gaps
- I found no official ICP/GDRFA/MOHRE source on fines for working on a visit visa. It is widely understood to be illegal, but this was not sourced in this session.
- Saudi (Qiwa/iqama) and Qatar (QID) visa steps were not researched.

## 5. Phrasing "licence in progress" in CV/cover letters

### Takeaway
No recruiter source was found. The official DHA process gives precise, verifiable vocabulary. Use the official stage names and DHA title, and never imply a licence is active before activation (DHA clause 7.2 forbids non-licence titles).

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** These are the official stage names to use:
  1. Sheryan self-assessment (eligible result).
  2. Primary Source Verification (DataFlow) report.
  3. CBT (Prometric) passed.
  4. Professional Registration Certificate, valid 1 year, listed on the Dubai Medical Registry.
  5. Licence activation by the hiring DHA-licensed facility. — [DHA Licensing Manual PDF](https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Manual%20for%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20professionals-202420241212775.pdf)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Titles other than the DHA licence title (e.g. "Aesthetician", "Beauty Expert", "Cosmetologist") should not be used. — [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)
- **[COMMERCIAL job ads]** Employers phrase the requirement as "DHA license is a MUST", "DOH/DHA eligibility" or "DHA licensed Semi-permanent and microblading Artist". — [Qureos](https://app.qureos.com/jobs/permanant-make-up-artist-microblading-lip-blush-eyeliner-1758139923996); [MNC Jobs Gulf](https://www.mncjobsgulf.com/jobs/beauty-therapist-doh-dha-eligibility-abu-dhabi-1795788)

### Inferences
These are suggested wordings based on the official stages, not recruiter-validated:
- "DHA Beauty Therapist licensing in progress: Sheryan eligibility confirmed; DataFlow PSV submitted [month/year]; Prometric CBT booked [date]."
- After registration: "DHA registered (Beauty Therapist) – registration no. …, valid to [date]; ready for licence activation by employer."
- Abu Dhabi: "Prepared to obtain QCC Semi-Permanent Makeup Technician conformity certificate."
- Avoid "DHA licensed" until activation. Avoid "DHA approved" for the training certificate.

### Gaps
- No recruiter or industry quotes were found on how "in progress" status is received.

## 6. Pay: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Doha vs Norway

### Takeaway
Reliable PMU-specific salary data is scarce. Dubai ads show a **basic salary of AED 4,000–7,000 per month plus commission**, with the commission percentage unstated. Some ads offer commission-only. Generic makeup-artist averages are about AED 4,400 per month. I found no sourced data on housing, transport or flight allowances, and none for Riyadh or Doha salaries. Norway: about NOK 4,000–6,000 per brow treatment (undated article). The broad Norwegian make-up artist pay range is EUR 28.8k–52.2k per year.

## 7. Application norms in the GCC beauty market

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin. Sourced items: Dubai employers ask for a DHA licence, an accredited PMU certificate, years of experience, and a "proven portfolio" (often via social media link). Industry advice says to emphasise **healed** results. I found no sources on CV photos, trade tests/model days, WhatsApp hiring or notice periods.

### Cited Findings
- **[JOB AD]** Dubai PMU requirements: DHA licence, accredited PMU training certificate, 5 years' experience, proven portfolio, colour theory, facial anatomy, sanitation, English. — [Qureos](https://app.qureos.com/jobs/permanant-make-up-artist-microblading-lip-blush-eyeliner-1758139923996)
- **[COMMERCIAL]** A Dubai PMU guide lists DHA licensing, PMU certifications from recognised institutions, bloodborne pathogen training and liability insurance as expected. — [browsandlips.ae](https://browsandlips.ae/blog/semi-permanent-makeup-dubai-complete-guide-2026/)
- **[JOB AD, US]** Employers want before/after photos showing depth, mapping/symmetry, shape, colour selection and **healed results**. — [Glassdoor job listing](https://static.glassdoor.be/Job/lash-tech-jobs-SRCH_KO0,9.htm)
- **[OFFICIAL]** DHA requires the training certificate to be in-person, from an accredited institution, and to state CPD hours. Certificates such as PhiBrows should be checked against this. — [DHA Standard PDF](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Standards%20for%20Non%20Surgical%20Cosmetic20241243161.pdf)

### Inferences
- Cover letters and outreach should lead with three things:
  1. DHA status, using the official stage wording.
  2. Years of PMU experience and techniques (brows, lips, eyeliner, removal), which map to DHA Appendix 1 row 28.
  3. A link to a portfolio of **healed** results.
- Avoid the word "tattoo" with Saudi employers.
- CV photos, WhatsApp-first contact and trade tests are widely reported as common in the Gulf, but this was **not sourced** in this research. The agent should not assert them as facts.

### Gaps
- No sources found on: CV photo norms, trade tests/model days, WhatsApp as a hiring channel, typical notice periods (UAE labour law allows 30–90 days; not sourced here), or effective cold-outreach templates for clinics.
- No PMU forum or blog accounts from artists who relocated to Dubai were found in this session.
