# PMU employers and beauty recruiters: Saudi Arabia (Riyadh, Jeddah) and Qatar (Doha), as of Nov 2026

Method note: around 25 searches and fetches. Most of the PMU provider data comes from Fresha booking-directory pages, because individual salon websites rarely came up in search. Fresha shows services and prices, not hiring practices. Only one employer careers page (Brau) and one general business email (Celebrity Beauty Center) were confirmed. **No careers-specific email address was found for any employer or agency.** The large groups named in the brief (Dr. Sulaiman Al Habib, Kaya, Novomed, NStyle, Tips & Toes, Al Emadi, Elite, Dermacare) could **not** be confirmed as offering PMU. A search covering them returned nothing brand-specific, and none of their sites were fetched. Do not list them as PMU employers until someone has checked them.

## Which Riyadh/Jeddah salons, beauty centres and clinics offer PMU?

### Takeaway
In Saudi Arabia, PMU is mostly offered by independent women's salons and boutique studios, plus a few aesthetic clinics. Large dermatology groups do not seem to be the main providers. Brau (The Canopy, Riyadh) has a live-format ATS careers page for an SPMU Artist, and The Pink Tribe (Riyadh) advertised a dedicated PMU role at SAR 10,000/month. These two are the strongest hiring leads.

## How do they recruit, and which recruit European artists?

### Takeaway
The confirmed channels are an employer ATS (Brau), Saudi job boards (Sabbar, Bayt, Jooble, Glassdoor, Jobrapido), and agency or board postings (Career Land Center, SpaStaff). Most salons seem to hire through WhatsApp and Instagram, but I could not confirm this for specific salons. No employer explicitly says it seeks European artists, though "European salon" branding is common in Doha.

### Cited Findings
- Brau hires through its own ATS at career.brau.ae; no email is given. — [Brau careers](https://career.brau.ae/jobs/7656771-spmu-artist-riyadh)
- Riyadh PMU roles appear on Sabbar (The Pink Tribe), Glassdoor ("Werk-Brau" SPMU Artist, open more than 30 days), Bayt and Jobrapido ("Semi Permanent Makeup Artist – Riyadh"). — [Glassdoor Riyadh artist jobs](https://www.glassdoor.com/Job/artist-jobs-SRCH_IC3110290_KO0,6.htm); [Sabbar Riyadh makeup artist](https://sabbar.com/en/jobs/c-riyadh-r-makeup-artist-t-full-time?page=1); [Bayt Makeup Artist ME](https://www.bayt.com/en/international/jobs/makeup-artist-jobs/); [Jobrapido](https://sa.jobrapido.com/jobpreview/1696372008004091904)
- Saudi salon postings often require a transferable Iqama, which favours candidates already in the country. — [JobLeads Riyadh spa therapist](https://www.jobleads.com/sa/job/spa-therapist-female--riyadh--e8d58e0e3dd9ca13cf88f03d501037bca)
- Doha salon postings on Qureos and GulfTalent often require a NOC or transferable visa. Pay for general beauticians is low (around QAR 1,300–3,000/month, sometimes with accommodation and transport), and one listing says "Asian candidates preferred". — [Qureos Doha listings](https://app.qureos.com/jobs/all-rounder-beautician-asian-candidates-preferred-1772658367861); [Qureos eyelash beautician](https://app.qureos.com/jobs/eyelash-beautician-1760601606945)

### Inferences
- Specialist PMU roles pay much more than general beautician roles (e.g. SR 10,000 at The Pink Tribe compared with about QAR 1,300–3,000 for Doha all-rounders). The agent should present the user as a PMU specialist or trainer, not a general beautician.
- Salons that publish only WhatsApp or general email contacts should get a short portfolio message (Instagram link and before/after photos).

### Gaps
- No careers emails were found for any salon or clinic.
- I found no explicit statement from any employer that it recruits European artists.

## Which recruitment agencies place beauty/aesthetics staff in KSA and Qatar?

### Takeaway
I found few specialist agencies. Career Land Center and SpaStaff.com are the most active for salon and spa roles in both countries. Spa Elite and Dragon Recruiting have recruited for the GCC but have older postings. Qatar's Ministry of Labour publishes a list of licensed recruitment offices that can be used to vet agencies.

## Vision 2030, demand, and Saudization rules for salons

### Takeaway
Demand is growing: market reports estimate the KSA luxury salon market at about USD 440m (2024) with roughly 8.5% CAGR, and Vision 2030 is cited as a driver. However, Ministerial Resolution 141602 (Apr 2023, in force 24 Dec 2023) localises women's beauty salons. It sets 100% Saudization for some roles and requires at least one Saudi woman at outlets with 10+ non-Saudi make-up or hair staff. Some occupations, such as nail and body care, are exempt. Whether PMU artists are covered is not stated anywhere I found.

### Cited Findings
- HRSD Ministerial Resolution No. 141602 (dated 2 Apr 2023 / 11 Ramadan 1444) localises women's beauty and dressmaking outlets, covering "women's salons" (activity code 960210). The compliance deadline was 24 Dec 2023. The localisation rate is calculated as Saudi staff divided by total staff per outlet. — [HRSD decision PDF](https://prod.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2023-05/For%20the%20Decision%20to%20Localize%20Women%27s%20Beauty%20and%20Dressmaking%20Outlets.pdf) (the fetch failed with a DNS error; details come from a search summary); [HRSD regulation page](https://hrsd.gov.sa/en/knowledge-centre/decisions-and-regulations/regulation-and-procedures/581444)
- 100% Saudization applies to branch manager and deputy branch manager roles at women's beauty and tailoring outlets. Littler's wording also suggests 100% for ladies' beauty salons in general, which is ambiguous. Outlets with 10+ non-Saudi workers in make-up artistry, hair care or tailoring must employ at least one Saudi woman. Exceptions include cleaning, nail care, body care, transport and delivery. — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/saudi-arabia-saudization-requirements-announced-for-several-activities-and-professions.html); [Littler](https://www.littler.com/publication-press/publication/littler-global-guide-united-arab-emirates-q2-2023)
- The ministry expected localisation of women's salons and sewing to create about 7,000 jobs. — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/632068)
- Historically, the sector relied heavily on expatriate women. A 2013 Eastern Province Chamber study found about 10% of salon workers were Saudi, with roughly 40,000 jobs held by non-Saudis. Salon owners reported visa and sponsorship-transfer problems and labour raids. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/news/486711); [Al Bawaba](https://www.albawaba.com/business/labor-raids-saudization-538002)
- Market: the KSA luxury salon market was about USD 440m in 2024 with an 8.53% CAGR forecast for 2025–2033, driven by Vision 2030, incomes and women's workforce participation (vendor report). — [UnivDatos](https://univdatos.com/reports/ksa-luxury-salon-market). Estimates for the broader beauty market conflict: IMARC gives USD 4.6bn (2024) and 3.7% CAGR, while L'Oréal calls it a USD 2bn market. — [IMARC](https://imarcgroup.com/saudi-arabia-beauty-personal-care-market); [Financial Express on L'Oréal](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/print/loreal-taps-saudi-arabia-as-global-beauty-innovation-launchpad-1759423904)
- L'Oréal's hairdressing academy in Dammam trains Saudi women for salons; 67% of graduates were placed. This is evidence of a push to build Saudi talent. — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/loreal-saudi-arabias-hairdressing-academy-celebrates-inaugural-graduation-of-imam-abdulrahman-bin-faisal-university-in-dammam-azoqmpay)
- Fresha (via AstroLabs) reports fast digital adoption by Saudi salons. — [AstroLabs](https://insight.astrolabs.com/fresha-capitalizing-on-the-growing-glow-of-the-saudi-beauty-sector)

### Inferences
- Foreign PMU artists can still be hired, but salons must meet outlet-level Saudi ratios. Every outlet needs Saudi managers, and larger outlets need at least one Saudi woman in make-up or hair roles. A senior foreign artist who also trains Saudi staff (as in Brau's listing) is a strong pitch under these rules.
- Qatar has no comparable salon nationalisation rule in what I found. The main constraints there are MoPH licensing for cosmetic-medical procedures and visa/NOC terms.

### Gaps
- The HRSD occupational table could not be read (the PDF fetch failed). I cannot confirm whether "permanent makeup / tattoo artist" falls under the localised "make-up artist" occupation or is exempt.
- I found no 2025–2026 data showing whether Saudi women's salons are hiring more international staff since the 2023 rule.
- Saudi Ministry of Health or SCFHS licensing for cosmetic tattooing was not researched.
